Our Story

Indian ornament,
made for today.

The idea

Solvaara began with a simple question: what would traditional Indian ornament look like if it were designed today? Our answer is jewellery that keeps the beauty of the old forms and drops everything unnecessary.

Our approach

Ornament, reduced to its essence.

Traditional jewellery is rich in pattern. We study those patterns, keep what gives a piece its character, and simplify the rest, so each design feels calm, balanced and easy to wear every day.

verified Honest details

We list only details we can stand behind: what a piece is made of, its size, and whether it is available.
